# Demo Car 2026 Owner's Manual (fictional excerpt for evaluation only)

This manual is invented for testing. It does not describe any real vehicle.

## Tyres
- Tyre pressure when cold: front 2.5 bar, rear 2.3 bar.
- Check the pressure at least once a month.

## Fuel
- Use unleaded petrol with at least RON 95. E10 is allowed.
- The fuel tank holds 55 litres.

## Warning lights
- If the oil warning light comes on while driving, stop the car as soon as it is safe, switch off the engine and check the oil level.
- Do not drive a long distance with the oil warning light on.

## Maintenance
- Service interval: every 15,000 km or 12 months, whichever comes first.

## Electric charging
- For daily use, charging the battery to 80 percent is recommended.
- Fast charging is supported at DC charging stations.

## Washer fluid
- Top up with washer fluid.
- In cold weather, do not use plain water, because it can freeze.

## Child seats
- Children under 12 should sit in the rear seats.
- Do not install a rear-facing child seat on the front passenger seat when the passenger airbag is active.

## Hazard warning lights
- Press the button with the red triangle symbol on the centre console.

## Winter tyres
- For winter tyres, raise the tyre pressure by 0.2 bar above the standard values: front 2.7 bar, rear 2.5 bar when cold.

## Long journeys
- Before a long trip, charge the battery to 100 percent just before departure. For daily use, keep to 80 percent.
- If the brake warning light comes on, stop safely and check the brake fluid level. Do not drive on until the cause is checked.

## Scheduled items
- Brake fluid is replaced every 30,000 km. This is in addition to the regular service every 15,000 km.

## Front passenger airbag
- A forward-facing child seat may go on the front passenger seat only if the passenger airbag is switched off with the airbag key switch.
- A rear-facing child seat must never be placed on the front passenger seat while the passenger airbag is on.
